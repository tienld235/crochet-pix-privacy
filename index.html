Privacy Policy & Pro Terms

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Crochet Pix (by TieChan Software) turns a photo into a crochet chart on your phone. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location. Google AdMob, which shows the ads, does collect some data — see Advertising below.

Your photos stay on your phone

You choose a photo with the system photo picker (the app cannot see your other photos) or take one with the camera. Selecting the subject, removing the background and building the chart all happen on your device. Background removal uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone; its model is downloaded once through Google Play services, and your photo is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

For each chart you create, the app keeps the chart itself, your progress (the row you are on) and a small copy of the area you selected, so you can adjust the chart later. It also remembers your language, units and settings. All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. A PDF or chart image is written to your phone's Download or Pictures folder only when you tap Save. Deleting a project deletes its copy immediately; uninstalling the app deletes everything. We never receive a copy.

Permissions

Camera: to take a photo you want to turn into a chart. Storage (only on Android 9 and older): to save a PDF or chart image to your phone.

Purchases

Crochet Pix Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card, wallet or bank details. Google Play tells the app which Pro product your Google account owns, so the app can turn Pro on and restore it after you reinstall.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of some screens, a full-screen ad after you create a chart (never while you are crocheting), and a short ad you can choose to watch to unlock a large chart or to save a PDF. Pro removes all ads. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID and technical information about your device. This is handled by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

What Google may collect

An advertising identifier and app set ID (resettable IDs, not your name); your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location; app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views; and diagnostic and device information, such as device type, operating system version, app launch time and hang rate. Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and it is sent over encrypted connections.

Users in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before your data is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Data security

Crochet Pix has no server and no account, so we never receive or store your photos or your data anywhere. Your charts and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The advertising data described above is collected and sent by Google's AdMob SDK over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and handled under Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your charts stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app; each of these deletes them immediately. Files you saved to Download or Pictures stay there until you delete them. We hold no copy that would need deleting.

Crochet Pix Pro terms

Pro is offered as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. The monthly subscription renews automatically each month through Google Play until you cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next renewal and Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment that unlocks Pro for as long as the app is offered, on the Google account that bought it. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. We may change the price of the subscription in the future; Google Play will notify you in advance as its rules require, and a price increase never applies to a period you have already paid for. Chart sizes are estimates — real size depends on your yarn, hook and tension.

Children

This app is rated for users 13 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Crochet Pix Pro dijual lewat Google Play. Pembayaran sepenuhnya ditangani Google Play; kami tidak pernah melihat data kartu, dompet digital, atau rekening bankmu. Google Play memberi tahu aplikasi produk Pro mana yang dimiliki akun Google-mu, supaya aplikasi bisa mengaktifkan Pro dan memulihkannya saat kamu memasang ulang.

Iklan

Versi gratis menampilkan iklan lewat Google AdMob: banner di bagian bawah beberapa layar, iklan layar penuh setelah kamu membuat pola (tidak pernah saat kamu sedang merajut), dan iklan singkat yang bisa kamu pilih untuk ditonton agar bisa membuka pola berukuran besar atau menyimpan PDF. Pro menghapus semua iklan. Untuk menayangkan iklan, Google dapat mengumpulkan ID iklan perangkatmu dan informasi teknis tentang perangkat. Ini ditangani oleh Google, bukan kami. Kamu bisa mereset atau menghapus ID iklan di Setelan Android → Privasi → Iklan. Kebijakan Google: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Apa yang mungkin dikumpulkan Google

ID iklan dan ID set aplikasi (ID yang bisa direset, bukan namamu); alamat IP-mu, dipakai untuk memperkirakan lokasi kasarmu; interaksi dengan aplikasi, seperti membuka aplikasi, ketukan, dan iklan yang dilihat; serta informasi diagnostik dan perangkat, seperti jenis perangkat, versi sistem operasi, waktu buka aplikasi, dan tingkat macet. Google memakai data ini untuk menampilkan dan mengukur iklan, untuk analitik, dan untuk mencegah penipuan, dan data dikirim lewat koneksi terenkripsi.

Pengguna di Eropa

Jika kamu berada di Kawasan Ekonomi Eropa, Inggris, atau Swiss, aplikasi akan meminta persetujuanmu terlebih dahulu (dengan formulir dari Google) sebelum datamu dipakai untuk iklan yang dipersonalisasi. Kamu bisa mengubah pilihan kapan saja di Pengaturan → Opsi privasi iklan. Jika kamu tidak setuju, kamu tetap bisa melihat iklan, tetapi tidak dipersonalisasi.

Keamanan data

Crochet Pix tidak punya server dan tidak memakai akun, jadi kami tidak pernah menerima atau menyimpan foto dan datamu di mana pun. Pola dan pengaturanmu disimpan di penyimpanan pribadi aplikasi di perangkatmu, yang tidak bisa dibaca aplikasi lain. Data iklan yang dijelaskan di atas dikumpulkan dan dikirim oleh SDK AdMob milik Google lewat koneksi terenkripsi (HTTPS) dan diproses sesuai kebijakan privasi Google.

Penyimpanan dan penghapusan data

Polamu tetap ada di perangkat sampai kamu menghapusnya di aplikasi, menghapus data aplikasi, atau mencopot aplikasi; cara mana pun langsung menghapusnya. File yang kamu simpan ke Download atau Pictures tetap di sana sampai kamu menghapusnya. Kami tidak menyimpan salinan apa pun yang perlu dihapus.

Ketentuan Crochet Pix Pro

Pro tersedia sebagai langganan bulanan atau pembelian sekali seumur hidup. Langganan bulanan diperpanjang otomatis setiap bulan lewat Google Play sampai kamu membatalkannya di Google Play → Pembayaran & langganan → Langganan; membatalkan menghentikan perpanjangan berikutnya dan Pro tetap aktif sampai akhir periode yang sudah kamu bayar. Pembelian seumur hidup adalah pembayaran sekali yang membuka Pro selama aplikasi masih tersedia, di akun Google yang membelinya. Pengembalian dana mengikuti kebijakan pengembalian dana Google Play. Kami dapat mengubah harga langganan di masa mendatang; Google Play akan memberitahumu lebih dulu sesuai aturannya, dan kenaikan harga tidak pernah berlaku untuk periode yang sudah kamu bayar. Ukuran pola hanya perkiraan — ukuran sebenarnya tergantung benang, hakpen, dan kekencangan rajutanmu.

Anak-anak

Aplikasi ini ditujukan untuk pengguna berusia 13 tahun ke atas dan tidak ditujukan untuk anak-anak.
